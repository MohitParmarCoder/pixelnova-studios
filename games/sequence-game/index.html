HOW TO PLAY
Code Breaker
👆TAP TO PLAY
  • The computer picks a secret sequence of 4 colors
  • Tap each slot to cycle through the 6 colors for your guess
  • Tap SUBMIT — black peg = right color and place; white peg = right color wrong place
  • You have 8 guesses to crack the code!
