HOW TO PLAY
LAVA SURF
👆TAP TO PLAY
  • Tap left or right to adjust your aim angle
  • Tap RELEASE to launch the stone down the ice
  • Stone slows to a stop — closer to bullseye = more points
  • 6 stones per game — highest total wins!
