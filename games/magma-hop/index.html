HOW TO PLAY
MAGMA HOP
👆TAP TO PLAY
  • Your frog starts at the bottom — reach the top safe zone!
  • Tap upper half to hop forward one lane
  • Tap lower half to hop back; tap sides to move left/right
  • Getting hit by a car or falling in the river loses a life!
